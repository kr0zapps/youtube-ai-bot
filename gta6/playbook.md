# GTA 6 Faceless Clipping Page — Playbook (SOP)

## 1. Account Info & Brand Identity
- **Niche:** Grand Theft Auto VI (Official news, high-utility HOW-TOs, trailer breakdowns, gameplay clips & countdowns).
- **Tone & Voice:** Authoritative yet energetic, hype-driven but 100% grounded in verified facts. Zero clickbait lies, zero spammy begging for follows.
- **Goal:** Build compound trust through speed on official announcements, high-utility tips that people save, and clean credited clips.

## 2. Daily Schedule (Adjust to Local Timezone)
- **Start of Day:** Check official sources (Rockstar Newswire, PlayStation Blog, Take-Two SEC filings) and overnight trends. Check Buffer queue for gaps and duplicates.
- **Morning Post:** Publish official news recap / countdown / utility breakdown. Prepare and schedule clips for the day.
- **Every 2–3 Hours:** Quick scan for breaking official updates. 15-minute engagement block (reply to comments, quote official posts, thank creators).
- **Evening Post:** Night post (countdown, community question, or gameplay analysis). Read metrics for posts from 12h, 24h and 72h ago. Update `scoreboard.csv`.
- **End of Day:** Short check-in report: what posted, top post with numbers, today's lesson, tomorrow's single-variable test.

## 3. Uploading & Scheduling (Buffer / Direct API)
- **Channels:** TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X (Twitter).
- **Rule 1 (Direct Video File):** Buffer requires a direct, public, permanent HTTPS MP4 URL. Never a local file link or expired link.
- **Rule 2 (Per-Platform Composer Tweaks):**
  - **X:** Remove hashtags completely. Short, punchy copy + link or native video.
  - **Instagram Reels:** Add 3 to 5 targeted niche hashtags (`#GTA6`, `#RockstarGames`, `#GrandTheftAuto6`), set cover frame, share to feed. Use "Trial Reels" when testing formats on non-followers.
  - **TikTok & YouTube Shorts:** Hook title in first 3 words, 3 high-volume tags (`#gta6`, `#gaming`, `#fyp`).
- **Rule 3 (Breaking News):** Use "Share now" instead of queue. Speed beats perfect scheduling slots.
- **Rule 4 (Fallback):** If video upload fails, post caption with link/quote to original post. Never post a clip without credit.

## 4. Launch Day "Be First" Playbook
- **Confirmed Info:** Launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. PlayStation unlocks at midnight local time.
- **Time Zone Edge:** New Zealand and Australia unlock first, many hours before the Americas. That is where first legit gameplay originates.
- **Before Launch:**
  1. Build watchlist: Official accounts, top streamers in NZ/AU/UK, r/GTA6 mods.
  2. Pre-make templates: "OUT NOW" graphics, vertical crop presets, caption styles, initial HOW-TOs (storage size, preload instructions, unlock hours).
  3. Clear queue around midnight unlock so live content is never delayed.
- **At Unlock:**
  1. Post "GTA 6 IS OUT NOW" the second it is live in the first region.
  2. Monitor NZ/AU streams. Clip first strong moments (avoid story spoilers; prioritize driving, physics, NPC reactions, graphics, map scope).
  3. Edit in 15–30 minutes: hook, vertical trim, captions, source credit.
  4. Post to X first, then Reels, TikTok, Shorts.
  5. Post every 1–2 hours for the first 48 hours.

## 5. Engagement Routine (2–3 times a day, 15 min)
- Reply to comments within the first 60 minutes.
- Reply under official Rockstar / PlayStation tweets with verified facts or insightful details.
- Tag and thank creators when clipping their highlights (they often repost or engage).
- Save recurring questions directly into `idea-bank.md`.
- Zero arguing, zero leak drama, zero DM spam.

## 6. Safety & Accuracy Rules (Strict Compliance)
1. **Official Sources Only:** Every claimed fact must be sourced from Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony PlayStation, or Microsoft Xbox. Log link in `facts.md`.
2. **No Fake News / Clickbait:** Never invent release dates, prices, map sizes or features.
3. **Rumors:** Default is OUT. If explicitly authorized, prefix with `[RUMOR]` and never use stolen materials.
4. **Zero Leaks & Exploits:** Never post stolen source code, hacked builds, or "play early" scams.
5. **No AI-Generated Fake Gameplay:** Never pass off Midjourney/AI video renders as real GTA 6 footage.
6. **Not Affiliated:** Clearly state fan/clipping page status in bio. Never impersonate Rockstar.
7. **Same-Day Takedowns:** If a creator or rights holder requests removal, delete immediately and log in `takedowns.md`.

## 7. Learning Loop (Optimization)
- Log all posts into `scoreboard.csv`.
- Exactly **one test per day** (single variable: hook phrasing, caption length, music volume, clip pacing).
- Minimum 24h before judging a post; prefer 72h read.
- Record every weekly review in `learning.md`. Double down on pillars above median; kill or fix pillars below median.
